Why Farm2Door

A shorter, fairer road from field to fridge

Three things change when you buy direct from farmers instead of through five layers of mandi.

Farmers earn more

By selling straight to you, farmers on Farm2Door keep an average of 63% of the final price — nearly double the mandi norm.

You know exactly where it's from

Every order comes with a trace code linking back to the farm, the harvest date, and the route it travelled to reach you.

It's fresher, faster

Most crates reach you within 48–72 hours of harvest — picked, sorted and delivered without sitting in a warehouse.
